Privacy Policy

Voxl · last updated 5 November 2026

The short version

Voxl does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Camera, LiDAR and your scans

The camera and LiDAR scanner are used only while you scan. Photos, depth data and the 3D models made from them are processed and stored only on your device, inside the app. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Files leave your device only when you choose to export or share them, using the standard iOS share sheet, to a destination you pick.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime unlock are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email or payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to those purchases.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
