What you can do

Spaces in 3D

Walk around a room, an apartment or a backyard. Get a photo-textured 3D model to orbit, view from the top, or walk through with a joystick.

Walls, doors, windows and the total area, recognised automatically. Export a clean PDF, PNG, SVG — or DXF for your CAD software.

How it works

From walk-around to 3D in minutes

Scan

Move slowly around the space or object. A live mesh and point view show what's already captured, and a soft chord tells you when new surface comes in.

Build

Voxl fuses LiDAR depth into a mesh and paints it with the sharpest photos — on the iPhone, nothing is uploaded.

Use it

Measure, place it in AR, cut out objects, prepare a print or export to your favourite 3D tool.

Support

Questions & help

Which iPhones does Voxl work on?

Scanning needs a LiDAR scanner: iPhone 12 Pro, 13 Pro, 14 Pro, 15 Pro, 16 Pro, 17 Pro and later Pro models, with iOS 17 or newer. On other iPhones you can open, measure and export projects and try the built-in sample.

How do I get the best scans?

Move slowly, stay about 1–1.5 m from surfaces and turn the lights on — the texture comes straight from the photos. Cover floor, ceiling and corners, and come back to places you've already scanned. You can rebuild any scan later with more detail or more smoothing, without scanning again.

Can I scan outdoors?

Yes. Choose Space and scan as you would indoors. LiDAR reaches about 5 metres, and very bright sunlight can reduce its range a little.

How do subscriptions work?

New subscribers get a 7-day free trial. Payment is handled by Apple and charged to your Apple ID. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period — manage or cancel any time in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

I have a new iPhone — how do I get Pro back?

Open Voxl › Settings › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought Pro with.

Where are my scans stored? Can I move them to a computer?

Scans live only inside Voxl on your iPhone. Use Export to send models and plans to your Mac or PC (AirDrop, Files, email). Deleting the app deletes the scans, so export anything you want to keep.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and choose Voxl.
