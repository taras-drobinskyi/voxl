Voxl

3D scans of rooms, floor plans and objects — on your iPhone

Requirements

Scanning needs an iPhone with a LiDAR scanner (iPhone 12 Pro or newer Pro model). Everything is processed on the device.

Subscriptions

Voxl Pro unlocks exports (USDZ, GLB, OBJ, STL, PLY), floor plan files (PDF, PNG, DXF, SVG) and 3D-print export. New subscribers get a 7-day free trial. Manage or cancel in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions. To move Pro to a new iPhone, tap Settings › Restore purchases in the app.

Tips

  • Move slowly and keep 1–1.5 m from surfaces.
  • Turn the lights on — the texture comes from the photos.
  • Use Change detail (rebuild) to try other detail and smoothing settings without scanning again.
